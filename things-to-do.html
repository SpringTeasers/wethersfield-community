Things to Do in Wethersfield

Rich history, open water, and beautiful landscapes — all in one town.

Wethersfield is known for its rich history and its outdoor activities. Whether you're here for an afternoon or a lifetime, there's plenty to see and do.

Attractions

Featured Attractions

Wethersfield Cove

A beautiful public boat launch providing access to the Connecticut River, along with historical exhibits. A favorite spot for time on the water and a look at the town's maritime past.

Historic Wethersfield

Old Wethersfield District

A well-preserved area that showcases 18th-century buildings and beautiful landscapes. Visitors can take walking tours through one of the most historic streetscapes in Connecticut.

CTvisit

Wethersfield Historical Society

Offers exhibitions on local history and culture, including maritime history — a great starting point for understanding the town's four-century story.

Wethersfield Historical Society

Plan Your Visit

  • On the water: Head to Wethersfield Cove for river access via the public boat launch.
  • On foot: Take a walking tour of the Old Wethersfield District's 18th-century buildings.
  • Indoors: Visit the Wethersfield Historical Society's exhibitions on local and maritime history.
