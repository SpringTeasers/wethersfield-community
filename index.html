Wethersfield, Connecticut

Welcome to Wethersfield

A charming New England town with a deep history and a vibrant, close-knit community.

Wethersfield has been home to generations of neighbors since 1634 — one of Connecticut's oldest towns. Today it blends that storied past with the everyday life of a thriving community of about 27,298 people.

About

Four centuries, one town

Wethersfield was settled in 1634, making it one of the oldest towns in Connecticut. The town played a significant role in the history of early colonial America, growing into a center for trade and agriculture.

Wethersfield was famous for cultivating red onions — a crop that contributed significantly to its economy in the past.
