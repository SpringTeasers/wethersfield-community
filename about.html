Our History

Wethersfield was settled in 1634, making it one of the oldest towns in Connecticut.

The town played a significant role in the history of early colonial America, growing into a center for trade and agriculture. Wethersfield was famous for cultivating red onions, a crop that contributed significantly to its economy in the past.

How Our Town Is Governed

Wethersfield operates under a Council-Manager form of government:

  • A Town Council of nine members, each serving two-year terms
  • A Town Manager who implements the policies set by the Council
